# Mekong v0.6 — Trade & Customs Clearance Management

## Delivered Changes

v0.6 introduces a native Trade & Customs Clearance module for Cambodia and cross-border Mekong corridors. It bridges commercial freight shipments with General Department of Customs and Excise (GDCE) regulations, ASYCUDA World Single Administrative Documents (SAD), and Council for the Development of Cambodia (CDC) Qualified Investment Project (QIP) duty-free exemption quotas.

* **CDC Duty-Free Exemption Master Lists (`cdc_master_lists`)**:
  * Formal QIP exemption master list registration (`qip_number`, `company_name`, `tin_number`, `approval_reference`, `effective_year`, `valid_from`, `valid_until`, `status`).
  * Supports real-world industrial reference data (e.g. *Xin Cai Fu Garments QIP # 211-26* under CDC Decision No. 0488/26).
* **Approved QIP Quota Items Registry (`cdc_master_items`)**:
  * Granular duty-free quota allocation tracking categories: `machinery_equipment`, `raw_materials`, `accessories`, `packaging`.
  * Original (Chinese/Khmer) and English descriptions, HS tariff codes, model/specifications, approved quantities, consumed quantities, remaining stock balances, and unit valuations.
* **ASYCUDA World SAD Declarations (`customs_declarations`)**:
  * Lodgement of Single Administrative Documents (SAD) with office code (`KHKAS` Sihanoukville Autonomous Port, `KHPNH` Phnom Penh Port / Dry Port, `KHBAT` Bavet Border, `KHPOI` Poipet Border).
  * Customs regimes: `IM 4` (Direct Import for Domestic Use with QIP Duty-Free Exemption), `IM 7` (Bonded Warehouse), `EX 1` (Direct Export), `IM 8` (Cross-Border Transit).
  * Four-tier GDCE Inspection Lanes:
    * **Green Lane**: Direct release / fast-track auto-clearance.
    * **Blue Lane**: Post-Clearance Audit (PCA) review.
    * **Yellow Lane**: Documentary check & valuation scrutiny.
    * **Red Lane**: Physical cargo inspection & container scanner verification.
  * Links directly to operational `shipments`, commercial invoices, and bills of lading.
* **Itemized Declaration Lines (`customs_declaration_items`)**:
  * Line-by-line customs valuation, HS codes, gross/net weights, tariff rates, and duty exemption amounts.
  * Direct linkage to approved CDC master items for automated quota cut-stock deductions.
* **Immutable Cut-Stock Deductions Ledger (`cdc_quota_deductions`)**:
  * Transaction-safe quota deduction ledger tracking every deduction reference (`CUT-2026-XCF-001`), quantity consumed, balance before, balance after, and executing user.
  * Strict boundary enforcement preventing overdrafts against approved CDC quotas.
* **Automated Shipment Lifecycle Synchronization**:
  * Transitioning declaration status to `cleared` or `released` automatically updates the parent shipment's `customs_status` to `cleared` or `released` and emits standard `CUSTOMS_CLEARED` and `CUSTOMS_RELEASED` milestones on the shipment timeline.
* **Executive Navy Blue UI Suite**:
  * `/customs`: Customs declarations dashboard with KPI cards (total SADs, cleared cargo, under inspection, duty savings USD, active CDC lists), lane breakdown buttons, and modal SAD lodgement dialog (`NewDeclarationDialog`).
  * `/customs/[declaration]`: ASYCUDA SAD document viewer, financial valuation breakdowns, item lines, and status transition modal.
  * `/customs/cdc`: CDC QIP Master List quota visualizer with interactive progress meters (percentage consumed and remaining) and approved item intake modal.
  * `/customs/deductions`: Immutable audit log of all cut-stock deductions across all declarations.

## Security & Multitenancy Guarantees

* **Tenancy-Scoped Composite Keys**: Composite foreign keys `(organization_id, id)` on all customs tables (`cdc_master_lists`, `cdc_master_items`, `customs_declarations`, `customs_declaration_items`, `cdc_quota_deductions`).
* **Locked Transaction Mutations**: All creations, transitions, and quota deductions execute inside `OrganizationActions::mutate` with row-level locks on the parent organization.
* **Audit Trail**: Every customs action writes immutable audit records (`cdc_master_list.created`, `customs_declaration.created`, `cdc_quota.deducted`, `customs_declaration.status_transitioned`, `customs_declaration.deleted`).
* **Adversarial Tenant Isolation**: Validated with feature tests ensuring cross-tenant requests return `404 Not Found`.

```sh
docker compose exec app php artisan queue:restart
```

```sh
make demo
```
