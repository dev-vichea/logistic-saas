# Mekong v0.3 — Freight Management & Multimodal Shipments

## Delivered Changes

v0.3 advances Mekong from a multi-tenant business directory into a connected, operational logistics management software ecosystem. It establishes the complete commercial-to-operational workflow from initial freight inquiries through to multimodal transit tracking.

* **Freight Requests (Commercial Intake)**:
  * Customer cargo and service inquiries specifying origin, destination, cargo specifications (pieces, weight, CBM, hazardous, temperature control), incoterms, and requested readiness/delivery dates.
  * Status lifecycle: `draft` $\to$ `submitted` $\to$ `reviewing` $\to$ `quoted` $\to$ `cancelled`.
* **Quotations & Revisions**:
  * Multi-currency line-item freight proposals (freight charges, terminal handling, customs brokerage, documentation fees).
  * Revision tracking (`R0`, `R1`, etc.) preserving audit history across price changes.
  * Formal status lifecycle: `draft` $\to$ `sent` $\to$ `accepted` $\to$ `rejected` $\to$ `expired`.
* **Commercial Bookings (Orders)**:
  * Commercial commitments converted from accepted quotations or booked directly with customer PO references.
* **Shipments (Multimodal Operational Master)**:
  * Master tracking numbers, carrier assignments, origin and destination facilities.
  * **Decoupled State Machines**: Operational status (`draft`, `planned`, `dispatched`, `in_transit`, `arrived_hub`, `out_for_delivery`, `delivered`, `closed`) is fully independent of Customs status (`not_applicable`, `pending_docs`, `declaration_drafted`, `under_inspection`, `cleared`, `released`) and individual leg statuses.
* **Cargo Manifest (`cargo_items`)**:
  * Detailed commodity tracking, package types, quantities, dimensions, container and seal numbers, gross/net weights, and volumes.
* **Multimodal Transport Legs (`shipment_legs`)**:
  * Sequential transit segments across pre-carriage, main carriage, on-carriage, and customs inspection.
  * Modes supported: Road haulage, ocean voyages, air cargo, and rail dispatch.
* **Multi-Company Collaboration (`shipment_participants`)**:
  * Assignment of external shippers, consignees, carriers, and customs brokers to the shipment with role-scoped data visibility.
* **Milestone Activity Feed (`shipment_milestones`)**:
  * Chronological event log with timestamps, terminal/location tags, exception flags, and automatic operational status progression.

## Concurrency & Tenant Security

* **Scoped Tenant Routing**: All freight routes are nested under `/api/v1/organizations/{organization}/freight/` and resolved via `ResolveOrganization` with an immutable `OrganizationContext`.
* **Composite Foreign Keys**: `(organization_id, ...)` foreign keys strictly enforce in PostgreSQL that bookings, customers, carriers, and locations cannot be cross-referenced or hijacked from other tenants.
* **Refined Concurrency Locking**: Administrative and setup mutations acquire `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on `organizations`; operational mutations lock the specific shipment record (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE` on `shipments`), avoiding global organization bottlenecks.
* **Adversarial Tests**: Comprehensive cross-tenant feature tests verify that foreign tenants cannot access, mutate, or link another company's freight requests, quotations, bookings, or shipments.

