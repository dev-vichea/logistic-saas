# Mekong v0.4 — Transportation & Fleet Dispatch

## Delivered Changes

v0.4 introduces operational fleet management, road haulage dispatching, and digital proof-of-delivery (POD) tracking into the Mekong Logistics SaaS ecosystem. It bridges commercial freight shipments (v0.3) with physical vehicle and driver execution on regional road corridors.

* **Fleet Asset Management (`vehicles`)**:
  * Vehicle registry tracking registration numbers (plates), vehicle types (`tractor_head`, `rigid_truck`, `van`, `container_chassis`, `flatbed_trailer`), ownership models (`owned`, `leased`, `subcontracted`), max payload weight (kg), volume capacity (CBM), and operational status (`available`, `on_trip`, `maintenance`, `decommissioned`).
* **Driver Roster & Qualifications (`drivers`)**:
  * Professional drivers tracking full names, mobile contacts, license classifications (`C`, `D`, `E`), license numbers, expiry dates, and duty status (`available`, `on_trip`, `off_duty`, `suspended`).
* **Dispatch Trips & Run-Sheets (`dispatch_trips`)**:
  * Operational haulage missions binding a vehicle, driver, and route corridor (origin hub, destination hub).
  * Run-sheet telemetry logging: start odometer, end odometer, fuel consumption, container numbers, and seal integrity checks.
  * Formal status lifecycle: `draft` $\to$ `dispatched` $\to$ `in_transit` $\to$ `arrived_at_destination` $\to$ `delivered` $\to$ `completed` $\to$ `cancelled`.
* **Shipment Cargo Leg Integration (`dispatch_trip_legs`)**:
  * Multi-tenant composite pivot associating dispatch trips with pending road haulage legs (`shipment_legs`) from freight shipments.
  * Allows multi-order consolidation and cross-docking on a single vehicle run.
* **Automated Operational Milestones**:
  * Dispatching a trip (`draft` $\to$ `dispatched`):
    * Updates vehicle and driver status to `on_trip`.
    * Advances attached shipment cargo legs to `in_transit`.
    * Emits automated `DEPARTED` shipment milestones.
  * Delivering a trip (`in_transit` $\to$ `delivered`):
    * Advances attached shipment cargo legs to `completed`.
    * Automatically advances shipment operational status to `delivered` when all legs complete.
    * Releases vehicle and driver status back to `available`.
    * Emits automated `DELIVERED` shipment milestones.
* **Digital Proof of Delivery (`proof_of_deliveries`)**:
  * Electronic delivery confirmations capturing recipient name, recipient phone, delivery timestamp, package count reconciliation (expected vs delivered packages), cargo condition (`intact`, `damaged`, `shortage`), receiver remarks, and audit signature log.
* **Interactive Frontend Dashboards & Run-Sheet Views**:
  * `/transport/trips`: Run-sheet tracking table with vehicle/driver badges, route corridor, container/seal tags, quick dispatch and POD actions.
  * `/transport/trips/[trip]`: Detailed dispatch control room view with odometer telemetry, driver details, attached shipment cargo manifests, and signed POD cards.
  * `/transport/fleet`: Vehicle registry with payload capacity metrics and asset status filtering.
  * `/transport/drivers`: Driver roster with license qualification tracking and duty statuses.

## Concurrency & Tenant Security

* **Scoped Tenant Routing**: All transportation endpoints are nested under `/api/v1/organizations/{organization}/transport/` and resolved via `ResolveOrganization` with an immutable `OrganizationContext`.
* **Composite Foreign Keys**: Strict PostgreSQL foreign keys `(organization_id, ...)` prevent assigning vehicles, drivers, or shipment legs belonging to other tenants.
* **Organization Transaction Locking**: Mutations utilize `OrganizationActions::mutate` to acquire an exclusive row-level lock (`SELECT ... FOR UPDATE`), preventing race conditions when assigning drivers and vehicles concurrently.
* **Adversarial Cross-Tenant Tests**: Validated with feature tests ensuring foreign tenants receive `404 Not Found` when attempting to access or dispatch another organization's trips, vehicles, or drivers.

Run migrations on your active environment:

```sh
docker compose exec app php artisan migrate --force

```
